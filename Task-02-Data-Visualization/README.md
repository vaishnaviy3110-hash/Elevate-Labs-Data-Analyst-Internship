# Task 2 - Data Visualization and Storytelling

## 📌 Objective

The objective of this task was to transform sales data into an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard using Tableau. The dashboard focuses on key business performance indicators, sales trends, regional profitability, customer segments, and product performance.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Tableau Public
- Superstore Dataset
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Design
- Business Analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The interactive dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

- **Total Sales:** $2,326,534
- **Total Profit:** $292,297
- **Total Orders:** 5,111
- **Profit Margin:** 12.6%

## 📈 Visualizations Created

The dashboard contains six visualizations:

1. **Monthly Sales Trend** – Shows sales performance over time.
2. **Sales by Category** – Compares sales across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
3. **Profit by Region** – Compares profitability across different regions.
4. **Profit by Sub-Category** – Identifies the most and least profitable sub-categories.
5. **Sales by Segment** – Compares sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
6. **Top 10 Products by Sales** – Highlights the highest-selling products.

## 🔎 Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes two interactive filters:

- **Region**
- **Category**

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard dynamically and compare business performance across different segments of the dataset.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Category Performance
Technology generated the highest sales at **$839,893**, followed by Furniture at **$754,748** and Office Supplies at **$731,893**.

### 2. Regional Profitability
The Central region generated the highest profit at **$110,799**, while the West region generated the lowest profit at **$39,865**, showing a significant regional profitability gap.

### 3. Sub-Category Profitability
Copiers generated the highest profit at **$56,094**, while Tables recorded the largest loss at **-$17,753**. Fasteners and Bookcases also recorded losses, highlighting areas that may require further pricing and cost analysis.

### 4. Customer Segment Performance
The Consumer segment generated the highest sales at **$1,170,660**, followed by Corporate at **$715,806** and Home Office at **$440,068**.

### 5. Top Product Performance
The highest-selling product in the Top 10 list generated **$61,600** in sales, while the tenth-ranked product generated **$17,030**, showing a noticeable difference between the leading and lower-ranked products.

## 🎯 Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides an interactive overview of sales and profitability performance. It helps identify strong-performing categories, regions, customer segments, and products while also highlighting areas with lower or negative profitability.
