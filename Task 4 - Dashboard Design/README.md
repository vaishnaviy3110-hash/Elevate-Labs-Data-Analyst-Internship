# Task 4 - Dashboard Design

## 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** created using **Tableau** to understand overall sales performance, monthly trends, product performance, and country-wise sales.

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of this task was to create an interactive dashboard that presents sales data in a simple and visual way and helps business stakeholders make better data-driven decisions.

## 📁 Dataset

**Dataset:** Sample Sales Data  
**Records:** 2,823 sales records

The dataset contains information about:

- Orders and sales
- Quantity ordered
- Products and product lines
- Customers
- Order dates
- Countries
- Deal size
- Order status

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Tableau** – Dashboard creation and data visualization
- **CSV** – Source dataset

## 📌 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard includes four important KPIs:

- **Total Sales** – Shows the overall sales generated.
- **Total Orders** – Shows the number of unique orders.
- **Units Sold** – Shows the total number of products sold.
- **Average Order Value** – Shows the average sales value per order.

## 📈 Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend
A line chart showing how sales vary across different months.

### 2. Sales by Product Line
A bar chart used to compare sales across different product lines.

### 3. Sales by Country
A bar chart showing the **Top 10 countries** based on sales.

### 4. Top 10 Products
A bar chart showing the **Top 10 products** based on sales.

## 🔎 Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes two filters:

- **Year**
- **Product Line**

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard according to their selected year or product line.

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Classic Cars** is the highest-selling product line, generating approximately **$3.92M** in sales.
- The **USA** is the top-performing country, with approximately **$3.63M** in sales.
- Product code **S18_3232** is the highest-selling product in the Top 10 list.
- Sales performance changes across different months, which helps identify monthly sales patterns.
- The dashboard makes it easy to compare product and country performance.

## 💼 Business Value

This dashboard can help stakeholders:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Identify monthly sales patterns
- Compare different product lines
- Identify important markets and countries
- Find top-performing products
- Make better data-driven decisions
